- (direction) User requests five ordered rule changes for the Duelyst repository: board, summon location, counterattack, mana, deck and hand.
- (capability) CAP-1: replace the 9x5 square board with a hex-tile battlefield; movement, range, and adjacency use six neighboring directions.
- (capability) CAP-2: normal allied unit summons are legal only on available tiles adjacent to the owning general, never merely adjacent to other allied units.
- (capability) CAP-3: remove the Duelyst counterattack rule; being attacked does not automatically cause a return attack.
- (capability) CAP-4: each player starts with 20 reserve mana; at turn end transfer up to 3 from reserve to spendable mana, bounded by remaining reserve; cards spend mana; unspent mana persists without a 9-mana cap.
- (capability) CAP-4 opening exception: first player begins with 17 reserve and 3 spendable mana; second player begins with 16 reserve and 4 spendable mana.
- (capability) CAP-5: a deck has exactly one general and eight other cards; all eight cards begin in hand; remove draw, replace, and deck-exhaustion fatigue rules.
- (constraint) Apply the requested changes in order 1 board, 2 summon location, 3 counterattack, 4 mana, 5 deck and hand.
