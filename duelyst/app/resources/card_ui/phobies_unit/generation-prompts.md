# Active artwork provenance

The frame is coordinate-defined in frame_geometry.py. Do not reconstruct it from old AI card mockups. The material grain samples the original neutral_unit@2x.png top metal.

The following four source images are required by build_assets.py. They were produced using the built-in image_gen tool, and the final glyphs were cropped/exported by script.

- ui_icons_generated.png: gold sword, red heart, cyan boot, violet reticle, cyan wings, and cyan hourglass in sharp Duelyst-style painted pixel art. The unused old phase/wall cell is ignored; never use it.
- move_phase_ghost_generated.png: cyan hooded ghost with bright eyes and spectral tail, matching boot/wings, transparent background. Used instead of the old phase/wall cell.
- attack_types_generated.png: gold bow with arrow and bronze/gold bomb with lit fuse, matching sword, transparent background.
- rarity_hex_crystals.png: five elongated faceted hexagonal crystal inlays, white, blue, purple, orange, turquoise. Common mask fits the full central metal slot; no circular rarity sprite or circular socket.

The original Duelyst unit sprite, unit shadow, mana gem, and silver-framed Phoenix Fire icon are referenced directly from project resources and are not AI redraws.
